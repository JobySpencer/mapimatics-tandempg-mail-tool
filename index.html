Mapimatics TandemPG Mail Tool (test)

A development test of a small workflow tool, by Mapimatics Consulting.

What it is

This tool helps a small tandem-flight business prepare thank-you emails for its customers. It is in development and is used privately by its developer. It is not a public service and is not offered to the general public.

What it does

  • Creates draft emails in the signed-in person's own Gmail account. The tool never sends email; a person reviews each draft and sends it from Gmail.
  • Creates folders in the signed-in person's own Google Drive, one per customer, to hold that customer's photos.
  • Reads photos from a folder that the Drive owner has explicitly shared with it.
  • In later versions, may use an AI model to suggest wording and photo choices, always for the person to review.

Your data

The tool only works with the Google account of the person who signs in, and only for the features above. See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed, how it is used, and how to remove access.
